About

Commune Café at Millenia Walk is a coffee-loving community of friends brought together by a humble cup of coffee. This cafe-bistro near Promenade MRT serves specialty coffee — flat whites, lattes, cappuccinos and mochas praised for their intense, fragrant flavour — together with waffles, cakes and brunch plates in a relaxed space with a hidden mezzanine level, popular with office workers and weekend visitors alike.
