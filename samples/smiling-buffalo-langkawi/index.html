Smiling Buffalo Cafe

Specialty coffee cafe in a tropical garden, Pantai Chenang · Open daily 8:00 AM–5:00 PM

About

Set within the Panji Panji boutique chalet property near Pantai Chenang, Smiling Buffalo Cafe is a wall-less, garden cafe surrounded by lush tropical greenery. It is known across Langkawi for specialty coffee — espresso drinks as well as V60 and Aeropress hand brews — along with all-day breakfast, handmade pasta, pancakes and waffles in a relaxed, rustic setting.
