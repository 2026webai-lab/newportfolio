Runes

Glasshouse cafe at Biopolis, one-north · Mon–Fri 8:00am–4:00pm

About

Runes is a spacious glasshouse cafe on Level 1 of the Wilmar International headquarters at Biopolis, one-north — a sister cafe to Glyph Supply Co. Floor-to-ceiling triangular windows, hanging plants and warm natural light make it a tranquil spot for work, with free Wi-Fi and power points throughout. The roastery serves short, fairly priced espresso drinks made on a single-origin Myanmar coffee, plus pastries, sourdough sandwiches, salads and build-your-own sushi bowls.
