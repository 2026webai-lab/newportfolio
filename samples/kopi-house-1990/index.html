About

Kopi House 1990 is a traditional Singapore coffee house at 728 Ang Mo Kio, serving the neighbourhood's breakfast and kopi breaks the old-school way. Drop by for a cup of kopi and local favourites in a no-frills kopitiam setting. Foodpanda delivery is also available.
