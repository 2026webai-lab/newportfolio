About

Average Service is a stylish brunch cafe and cocktail bar on Jalan Besar, a short walk from Farrer Park and Bendemeer MRT stations. Despite the name, the food is anything but average: Bangkok-viral thick slab bacon on creamy eggs and brioche sourdough, handkerchief pasta in mentaiko cream, torched maple toast and cube-shaped cakes, alongside specialty coffee and ceremonial-grade matcha. Walk-ins only.
