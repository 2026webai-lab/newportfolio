About

Gajeong Cafe is a Korean-style cafe in Jurong East, Singapore — 'gajeong' means 'family' in Korean, and the cafe brings that warm, homely feel to the neighbourhood. It is open daily from 09:00 to 20:30 for coffee, drinks and Korean cafe fare. Delivery is also available via Foodpanda.
