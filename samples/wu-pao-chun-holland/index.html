About

Wu Pao Chun Holland Avenue is the Holland Village home of the award-winning Taiwanese bakery founded by Master Baker Wu Pao Chun. Transparent display cases and the signature Champion Oven in the open kitchen let you watch the bakers at work. The bakery is famous for its Champion Breads — the Lychee Rose Royale, which won at the 2010 Masters de la Boulangerie after a 15-hour fermentation in lychee wine with rose petals, and the Red Wine Longan — alongside newer creations such as the Mango Boule and Blackberry Cheese Crown.
