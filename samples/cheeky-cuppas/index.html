Melbourne-inspired cafe in Upper Thomson · Wed–Fri 8:30am–4:30pm, Sat–Sun 9:00am–4:45pm

About

Cheeky Cuppas is a quaint, Melbourne-inspired takeaway cafe under an HDB block on Sin Ming Road in Upper Thomson. Founded by a couple recreating Melbourne coffee culture in Singapore, the cosy nook — all browns, beiges, potted succulents and a vinyl player — is best known for its Spanner drinks topped with housemade sweet cream, and hand-whisked matcha made with premium powder imported from Japan. Savoury sandwiches and seasonal chiffon bakes complete the menu.
