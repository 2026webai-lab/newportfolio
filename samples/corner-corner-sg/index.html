Corner Corner (Coffee Concept)

Japanese kissaten cafe on Duxton Road · Tue–Fri 11:00am–5:00pm, Sat–Sun 10:00am–3:00pm

About

Corner Corner is a daytime coffee concept inside RPM by D.Bespoke, the vinyl listening bar on Duxton Road. Modelled on a Japanese kissaten, the intimate space is lined with vinyl records and vintage bottles, with soft jazz playing as baristas serve seasonal pour-overs, cold brews and artisanal teas — no espresso drinks here. Japanese-style desserts such as nama cream roll cake and caramel pudding complete a slow, secret-spot experience in Tanjong Pagar.
