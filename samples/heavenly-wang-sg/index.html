About

Heavenly Wang is a home-grown Singapore cafe chain serving the local breakfast classics Singaporeans grew up with — crispy kaya toast, soft-boiled eggs and a strong cup of kopi. A familiar stop for breakfast, tea breaks and casual local meals throughout the day, with outlets across Singapore.
