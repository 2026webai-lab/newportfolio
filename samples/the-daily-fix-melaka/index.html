The Daily Fix Cafe

Heritage cafe on Jonker Street, Melaka · Open daily 8:45 AM–8:30 PM

About

Tucked inside a souvenir shop on Jonker Street, The Daily Fix Cafe is one of Melaka's best-loved heritage cafes. Set in a vintage shophouse with a leafy courtyard, nostalgic decor and old-school music, it is known for fluffy pancakes, specialty coffee and relaxed brunch dishes — a popular break during a walk through the Jonker Street heritage area.
