Afterwit

Mexican taqueria in Kampong Glam · Open daily 12:00pm–10:00pm

About

Afterwit is a halal-certified Mexican taqueria on North Bridge Road in Kampong Glam, created by the team behind the Arab Street cafe (working title). The kitchen serves gourmet tacos on house-made corn tortillas, burritos, quesadillas, nachos and burgers, paired with artisanal coffee in a colourful, indie cafe setting filled with Mexican posters and music. Signature plates include the BBQ Cola Tacos with pulled beef brisket and the Cheese Skirt Tacos with melted mozzarella.
