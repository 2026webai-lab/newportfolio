Danger Close Beverage Co.

Artisanal drinks stall at Alexandra Village Food Centre · Tue–Sat 12:00–2:30pm

About

Danger Close Beverage Co. is a one-man drink stall in Alexandra Village Food Centre, founded in 2017 by self-proclaimed tea nerd Calvin Ang. Its speciality is artisanal sparkling tea brewed from premium full-leaf Chinese tea and served on tap like craft beer — golden, ice-cold and crowned with a thick tea-foam cap — alongside espresso, nitro cold brew coffee and gongfu tea. Signatures include the lychee red tea Chinois and the citrusy Lady Grey.
