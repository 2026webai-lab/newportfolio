Clap Cafe

About

Clap Cafe is a compact, inviting cafe in Waterloo Centre, between Bras Basah and Bencoolen, founded by friends who wanted a place where people gather and ideas flourish. With its double-height ceiling, communal tables, gentle jazz and strong coffee, it is a favourite calm retreat for remote work. The owner bakes daily — look out for the Basque burnt cheesecake and the maple pecan tart — alongside signature lattes such as sea salt, houjicha and tiramisu.
