MAVRX Holland Village

Specialty coffee bar in Holland Village · Open daily 8:00am–5:30pm

About

MAVRX Holland Village is the second outlet of homegrown coffee specialist MAVRX Coffee, sharing a calm, wood-accented space with Sunday Bedding at Holland Road Shopping Centre — on the site of the former Thambi Magazine Store. Behind a sleek La Marzocco machine, baristas pour the house blend, sourced from Brazil with caramel and cocoa notes, as espresso drinks and filter coffee, with croissants and cakes to pair. Outdoor seats line the front for takeaway and slow sips alike.
