About

Tucked into a quiet residential street in Taman Krubong Perdana, Le Maison Cafe Malacca feels like being welcomed into a neighbour's home. The cafe serves freshly baked cakes and croissants, coffee, matcha drinks and light mains made with halal ingredients — a calm spot for breakfast, lunch, tea time or working on a laptop.
