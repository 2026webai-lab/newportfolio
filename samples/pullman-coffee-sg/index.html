Pullman Coffee

Cafe inside Pullman Bakery, Jurong Regional Library · Open daily 8:30am–8:30pm

About

Pullman Coffee — the Tachihara Coffee cafe inside Pullman Bakery — sits on Level 1 of Jurong Regional Library in Jurong East. The Hokkaido-inspired Japanese bakery cafe is a peaceful spot for reading or working, serving reliable hand-drip coffee and matcha alongside fresh, buttery Japanese-style breads and pastries at friendly prices. The all-day menu adds bagels and burgers, including the Premium Beef Burger and Herb Grill Chicken Burger.
