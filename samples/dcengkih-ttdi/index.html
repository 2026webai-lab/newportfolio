About

D'Cengkih Restaurant is a family-run Malay eatery in Taman Tun Dr Ismail known for authentic Johorean home-style cooking. Diners choose from a wide spread of nasi campur dishes, Laksa Johor, lontong, soto and nasi lemak, plus a dedicated cart of traditional kuih and desserts — flavours that remind regulars of home.
