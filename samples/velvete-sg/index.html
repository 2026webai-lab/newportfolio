Velveté Boutique Cafe & Nails

Cafe & nail salon at Alexandra Central · Open daily 2:00–11:00pm

About

Velveté is a bespoke nail salon, beauty cafe and wellness boutique on Level 3 of Alexandra Central Mall, next to IKEA Alexandra — a one-stop sanctuary for a revitalised self. Sip artisanal coffee or tea and enjoy homemade bakes such as banana cake and brownies while you are pampered with toxin-free gel nails, restorative hand spa treatments and nail art, all in a warm, aesthetically pleasing space designed for proper treats and chill time.
