Ipoh Coffee Shop

Morning kopitiam in Taman Ayer Keroh Heights · Tue–Sun 06:30–13:30

About

Ipoh Coffee Shop is a morning kopitiam in Taman Ayer Keroh Heights, Melaka, open from 06:30 to 13:30, Tuesday to Sunday. Its hawker stalls serve local breakfast favourites — chicken rice, laksa, wan tan mee and mee hoon kueh — making it a handy stop for an early, hearty start to the day.
