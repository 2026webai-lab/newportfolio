02How It Works

1. You send the source text

Word, Excel, HTML, JSON, CSV, Markdown or plain text — plus your audience, glossary or past translations if you have them. I confirm scope and price before starting.

2. Draft

English is translated in full by me. For other languages, an AI/machine draft is produced as the working base — fast, and never delivered as-is.

3. Native post-edit

Every sentence is checked against the source for meaning, then rewritten into natural Japanese. Terminology, names and register are kept consistent across the whole text.

4. Final QA

Proper nouns, numbers, dates, times and formatting are double-checked before delivery, in the format you asked for.

05Sample — What Native Review Does

Hotel copy, before and after

Source: “Our hotel is located in the heart of the old town, just a five-minute walk from the station. All rooms are equipped with free Wi-Fi and an electric kettle, and our staff will be happy to help you with restaurant reservations.”

Machine-literal: 「私たちのホテルは旧市街の中心に位置し、駅から徒歩わずか5分です。すべての客室には無料Wi-Fiと電気ケトルが装備されており、私たちのスタッフはレストランの予約を喜んでお手伝いします。」

After native post-edit: 「旧市街の中心に佇む当ホテルは、駅から徒歩5分。全室に無料Wi-Fiと電気ケトルを備え、レストランのご予約もフロントで承ります。」

The facts are identical. The polished version drops the translated feel — no repeated 私たち, no 装備されております — and uses the compact, guest-facing register Japanese hotel guests actually read.
